Everything you need to know before adding Jetstream to your iPhone.

What does Jetstream do?

Jetstream tracks the destinations you care about and brings flight price trends, flexible date windows, deal quality, weather, and other travel signals into one visual dashboard.

Is Jetstream free?

Yes. Jetstream is free to download from the Apple App Store.

Can I book flights in Jetstream?

You can review flight and airline options, then launch a flight search from a destination card when you are ready to explore availability.

Does the app collect my data?

Apple’s App Store privacy label states that Jetstream does not collect data from the app.

Where can I download Jetstream?

Jetstream is available now for iPhone on the Apple App Store.
