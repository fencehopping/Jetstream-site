What the app does

Jetstream turns flight prices into something travelers can actually read.

Most travelers only see a search result. Jetstream gives them a planning surface: saved destinations, a map of fare opportunities, price trend cards, trip timing, and value context in one place.

Campaign ideas

Partnership formats that make sense for travel creators.
