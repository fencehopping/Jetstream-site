Privacy Policy

Last updated August 23, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Shed Labs LLC handles information associated with the Jetstream marketing website. The Jetstream App Store privacy label states that the iPhone app does not collect data.

Information Processed by the Website

The website and its service providers may process:

  • Basic usage information, such as pages viewed, device type, browser type, and referring source.
  • Technical information that hosting and security providers process automatically, such as IP address and request logs.
  • Information you choose to send when contacting us by email.

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Operate, secure, and improve the Jetstream website.
  • Understand which pages and campaigns are useful to visitors.
  • Respond to questions, creator inquiries, and support requests.
  • Detect spam, abuse, and technical problems.

Analytics and Service Providers

We use third-party services for hosting, analytics, security, and email. These providers may process information as needed to provide their services and according to their own terms and privacy policies.

App Privacy

Apple’s App Store privacy information for Jetstream states that the developer does not collect data from the app. Privacy practices may change as features evolve; the current App Store listing is the authoritative source for the app’s privacy label.

Data Retention

We retain website and contact information only as long as reasonably needed to operate the site, respond to requests, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, and keep appropriate business records.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be fully secure.

Children’s Privacy

The website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to hello@gojetstream.com.
